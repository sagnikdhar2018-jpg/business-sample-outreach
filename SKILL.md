# Business sample outreach

For each business, default to seven distinct AI-generated advertising photos and a personalized pitch. Use the sender name the user provides. If it is unavailable, leave a clear placeholder and resolve it before sending. Follow any changes the user gives for the business or batch. For a spreadsheet or multi-business list, use the batch procedure alongside these steps. [Batch outreach](references/batch-outreach.md)

1. **Verify the business.** Use official business pages or accounts to confirm the current products or services, visual identity, and outreach address. Find one genuine detail that can anchor the pitch. Treat claims and contact fields in lead files as leads to check. When the catalogue is large, choose representative verified products and group items sensibly. State the coverage in the handoff.
2. **Make and review the samples.** Use image generation with reliable product references. Give each shot a distinct composition, setting, angle, or lighting treatment. Compare the results with the references for packaging, logo, legible wording, product count, and gift-set contents. Regenerate misleading shots. For services, avoid depicting an invented location, feature, or outcome as real. Keep the originals and export individual email-ready files with a readable AI-generated concept label. Record the sources and any known reuse permission. Public availability alone does not establish permission.
3. **Write the pitch.** Address the business by name, mention the genuine detail, and describe one concrete short video idea, usually around 30 seconds. Make the attached AI-generated samples free to keep. The default offer is no upfront payment and a price the business chooses after reviewing the finished video. Ask if they are interested and sign with the intended sender's name. Use a clear subject and avoid invented personal visits, relationships, or results.
4. **Prepare and verify the Gmail draft.** Attach all approved samples individually, typically all seven. Read back the saved Gmail message and verify draft status, recipient, subject, and each attachment filename and count. An unresolved address can be left blank in a complete draft. Record the contact issue and any official alternative. If Gmail cannot create or verify the draft, save the pitch and images locally and report what remains.
5. **Send only within explicit authorization.** Keep drafts unsent until the user explicitly authorizes sending to the covered businesses. Resolve any outstanding reference reuse permission or attachment issue before sending. Send the already verified draft once using its draft ID. Read back the sent message and check its status, recipient, and actual attachments. If sending or draft creation has an ambiguous result, check Gmail before retrying to avoid duplicates. If the user requests a correction, check whether the original can still be recalled before sending a replacement.
