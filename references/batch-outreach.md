# Batch outreach from a lead list

1. **Establish the list.** Use the primary lead sheet or list as the roster. Supplemental ranking, status, and pitch tabs may repeat the same businesses. Reconcile duplicate brands and keep a mapping to their original rows. Check Gmail for relevant drafts and previously sent outreach before creating messages. Verify a prior match by business and campaign when a shared inbox or similar name makes it ambiguous. Reuse applicable drafts and skip previously sent businesses unless the user asks to contact them again.
2. **Keep production traceable.** Track each business with its contact source, product reference URLs, creative direction, and permission status. Use separate folders and clearly named files by business. Research, generate, and review in manageable batches when helpful. Checkpoint completed work with the saved draft ID, message or thread ID, recipient, and attachment filenames. On a restart, reconcile with Gmail before resuming.
3. **Finish the complete draft for each business.** Confirm that the correct set of photos follows the business into Gmail, and check every saved attachment against its intended filename. When no outreach email can be verified, leave the recipient blank and retain the complete pitch and attachments. Record any official contact form or alternative channel in the index. An alternate route in the lead file does not authorize using it.
4. **Check the roster before handing it back.** Account for every original lead, including duplicate rows, prior outreach, finished drafts, missing addresses, and incomplete work. Verify that the attachment sets contain the expected files and that each new Gmail draft belongs to one business. Provide a single index for a large batch, with one entry per business and links to the appropriate Gmail thread or draft, sample files, and source notes when available. Mark unfinished items with the specific issue. Preserve the input lead sheet unless the user asks for edits.
5. **Apply send authorization to the exact batch.** Include only businesses the user explicitly authorizes that have verified recipients, complete reviewed attachments, and cleared reference permissions. Check Gmail for an existing sent message before retrying a draft with an uncertain send result. Report verified sends and the status of remaining leads.
